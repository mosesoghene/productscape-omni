## **Overview**  
**ProductScape Omni** is a comprehensive project and product lifecycle management platform designed exclusively for product managers. Built with Django, it empowers teams to **strategize, execute, and collaborate** seamlessly across every phase of a product’s journey – from discovery to sunsetting.  

Developed by [ProductScape](https://productscape.com), this tool integrates our proven methodologies for product strategy, training, and community-driven innovation into a single, intuitive platform.

---

## **Key Features**  
✅ **End-to-End Lifecycle Management**  
   - Ideation, roadmapping, launch planning, and retirement workflows.  
✅ **Strategy Templates**  
   - Pre-built frameworks for OKRs, prioritization, and customer discovery.  
✅ **Team Collaboration**  
   - Task boards, @mentions, and real-time updates for cross-functional teams.  
✅ **Metrics Dashboard**  
   - Track KPIs like NPS, retention, and revenue growth in one place.  
✅ **Community Integration**  
   - Share roadmaps, join masterclasses, and connect with mentors.
